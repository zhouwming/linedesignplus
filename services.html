Transmission Line Design & Engineering

All services leverage PLS-CADD, PLS-POLE, and PLS-TOWER for precision and compliance.

1. New Line Design

Full engineering from routing to staking charts, including:

  • Conductor sag-tension analysis
  • Structure spotting and loading
  • Clearance verification (NESC, OSHA, FAA)
  • Bill of materials and construction drawings

2. Line Upgrades & Reconductoring

Evaluation and redesign of existing lines for:

  • Increased capacity or voltage
  • Aging infrastructure assessment
  • Wind/ice load upgrades

3. Structural Analysis

Independent review or full modeling of:

  • Wood poles, steel poles, and lattice towers
  • Guys, anchors, and foundations
  • ASCE 7, NESC, and utility-specific standards

4. PLS Workflow Support

Custom templates, macros, and automation to streamline your team’s PLS-CADD usage.
